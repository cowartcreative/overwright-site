What it does

Everyday PDF work, minus the drama

Edit the original

Select the PDF’s own words, lines or images — move them, recolor them, cut and paste them to another page, or erase them cleanly.

Combine & organize

Merge PDFs in the order you choose. Reorder, rotate and delete pages. Open several documents in tabs and copy-paste between them.

Add text to any PDF

Click anywhere and write — any size, font, or color. White-out a line and retype it to fix wording on the original.

Sign & fill

Draw your signature with a mouse, trackpad, or finger — it’s remembered for next time — or place a scanned one. Form fields, checkboxes and dropdowns fill right in the app.

Mark up cleanly

Highlights, boxes, freehand pen, and ruler-straight lines that snap level — X out a section and have it look intentional. Stamps and page numbers included.

Stay in control

Find anything with ⌘F. Undo and redo everything. Your original file is never touched — saving always writes a new PDF wherever you choose.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Is my document really private?

Yes — structurally, not just as a promise. Overwright has no server component and works with your internet turned off. The only copy of your document is the one on your disk.

Does it change my original file?

No. Overwright always saves a new PDF wherever you choose; the file you opened is never modified. It also warns you before closing anything with unsaved changes.

Can it edit the PDF’s original text?

You can white-out and retype wording, and select, move, recolor or erase the original content. Moved or recolored content becomes a sharp image of itself — prints perfectly, but it’s no longer searchable text. Full text reflow like a word processor is not what Overwright does.

What about scanned PDFs?

View, sign, stamp, mark up, combine and reorganize scans freely. There’s no OCR yet, so a scan’s text can’t be selected word-by-word — the area-select tool covers that instead.
